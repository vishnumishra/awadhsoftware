About Awadh Software Solutions

Awadh Software Solutions started in 2014, when founder Vishnu Mishra shipped his first production app from a single-room office in Saketpuri, Ayodhya. Eleven years later, that single room has become a focused studio of engineers, designers, marketers and SEO specialists.

We chose to grow in Ayodhya on purpose. We don't take on more than we can do well, we tell clients honestly when an idea needs surgery, and we stay around long after launch — most of our work is with people we've worked with for years.

By the numbers

  • 11+ years of experience — founder-led since 2014
  • 50+ projects shipped, from MVPs to enterprise systems
  • Clients in 8 countries: India, US, UK, UAE, Singapore, Australia, Canada, Germany
  • Average first response: 24 hours — real humans, not bots

Our values

  • Sankalp · Commitment — when we say we will ship by a date, we ship. When we say a number is the budget, it is.
  • Satya · Honesty — we tell clients when an idea is wrong, and ourselves when work is not good enough yet.
  • Dharohar · Legacy — we build things meant to last. Code, relationships, reputation — all compound.
